DCTA · AUDIT LOG

Every step is written down, and nobody can quietly change it

Each entry (what you said, the draft, the checks, your signature, the payment) stores a fingerprint of the entry before it. Edit one old entry and its fingerprint no longer matches, so Verify points to the exact entry that was changed.

Press Tamper on any entry to see it. The change is made to a copy of the log, so the real log is never altered. Honest limit: this catches someone editing entries; someone who rewrites the whole chain would need an outside copy of the fingerprints to be caught.
